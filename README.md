## Why Regular Boat Engine Maintenance is Essential

Marine engines operate under continuous heavy loads and demanding freshwater and river conditions. Skipping seasonal maintenance can lead to overheating, poor acceleration, bad fuel economy, and costly internal repairs.

### Benefits of a Seasonal Marine Engine Tune-Up:
* **Enhanced Engine Reliability:** Prevents unexpected mechanical stalls on the water.
* **Improved Fuel Economy:** Clean spark plugs and fresh filters optimize fuel consumption.
* **Overheating Protection:** Inspecting and replacing worn impellers ensures optimal cooling flow.
* **Smooth Idling & Acceleration:** Fine-tuning ignition and fuel systems ensures fast startup.

## Our Comprehensive Marine Tune-Up Checklist

A complete marine tune-up covers every critical mechanical and electrical component of your outboard or inboard engine:

1. **Diagnostic Scanning & Computer Checks:** Identifying fault codes and operational history.
2. **Spark Plug Replacement:** Installing fresh spark plugs gapped to exact OEM specifications.
3. **Fuel System Inspection & Service:** Cleaning fuel injectors, replacing water-separating fuel filters, and checking fuel lines.
4. **Water Pump Impeller Replacement:** Servicing the cooling system to prevent high-temperature engine damage.
5. **Lower Unit Gear Lube Change:** Draining old gear oil, checking for water intrusion or metal shavings, and refilling with high-grade marine lube.
6. **Battery & Charging System Test:** Cleaning terminals, inspecting cables, and testing alternator performance.
